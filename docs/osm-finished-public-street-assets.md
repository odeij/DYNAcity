# Mapped street asset preparation

Three finished, unbranded CC0 Innerscene models were prepared using the existing
finished-asset converter on 27 October 2026. The downloaded GLBs were checked
against the SHA256 values on their original source pages; prepared manifests
also retain the FBX digest and source licence.

| Candidate | Source | Prepared mesh | Placement status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Steel bollard | [Innerscene](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/steel-security-bollard-5e948999) | 3,380 triangles; 0.9 m high | Eight source-node placements prepared using the generic model. Material and appearance are illustrative. |
| Concrete bollard | [Innerscene](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/concrete-site-bollard-6fddd9c2) | 3,648 triangles; 0.81 m high | Alternate model staged; the OSM records do not identify their materials. |
| Bus shelter | [Innerscene](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/bus-shelter-90077332) | 752 triangles; 4.84 × 1.77 × 3.04 m | Staged, but no mapped shelter was identified specifically as a bus shelter. Generic shelter and bus-stop records alone do not establish one. |

The models and manifests are private under
`C:/private/dynacity-data/engine/asset-quality-pass/`. The bollard manifest
`bollard-placements.json` is generated by `scripts/prepare_mapped_bollards.py`.
It uses only mapped bollard nodes with usable terrain-backed positions; it does
not create point locations for OSM ways or move source points. It records OSM
IDs, dates, terrain provenance and the prepared asset digest. The mapping is a
generic visual choice, not proof of observed physical appearance. Native Unreal
import and collision/visibility validation remain separate steps.
