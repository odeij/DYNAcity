Every fetched greenery source is represented within the extraction bounds.
Four source records only locate a park or wood with a point: small cartographic
rings retain those locations without inventing park outlines or trees. One scrub
way is unclosed: a thin reference stroke retains that geometry without filling
an invented area. Reference geometry is cartographic, not physical vegetation.

Additional supported families include landcover, hedges, shrubs, flowerbeds,
heath, village greens, nurseries, vineyards, wetlands, planters and green roofs.
Not every supported family occurs in this extract. Full botanical tags are
retained, and needle-leaved tags select a conifer presentation. Species, shape,
missing height and row spacing still include illustrative choices. Park/wood
polygons do not supply individual tree locations.

The original Beirut001 ground samples remain unchanged. A visual extension uses
filtered Copernicus GLO-30 elevations, resampled onto the existing 10 m grid;
the native data is roughly 30 m and source vertical alignment remains inferred.
The overlap median adjustment is 0.40 m, with a 7.84 m 90th-percentile absolute
residual. This is useful for visual placement and cannot establish street-level
ground accuracy. A remaining 467 m² mapped coastal scrub patch uses explicitly
recorded inverse-distance visual infill; nearest valid land samples are within
8.89 m. It creates no terrain outside that mapped patch.

Large source polygons may continue beyond the extraction boundary. The audit
distinguishes their full source area from modeled coverage inside the boundary.
Derived carriageway buffers are masked from green cover; width remains inferred.

## Sources and verification

Tag coverage follows the [OSM vegetation documentation](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Vegetation)
and [landcover documentation](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Palettes).
Conifer photographic textures come from [Poly Haven Pine Tree 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/pine_tree_01)
by Rico Cilliers and Rob Tuytel, CC0. Conifer mesh geometry is original and generic.

The export/import report is generated by `scripts/report_unreal_greenery.py`.
Private reports are under `C:/private/dynacity-data/engine/greenery-refresh/`:
`greenery-coverage.json` retains every source identity, extraction date,
representation, skipped reason and coverage figure; the matching Markdown file
summarizes categories. Native actor/instance checks are saved in the private
Unreal project's `Saved/DynaCity/greenery-native-verification.json`.

The update contains 173 completed surface/boundary/reference packages and 92
instance groups. Native verification checks their presence, instance counts,
duplicate cover labels and saving. Automated tests exercise tag semantics,
unclosed outlines, ground preservation, projection, triangulation and source IDs.
OSM real-world completeness and photorealistic species matching remain unknown.

After a Chaos physics access violation during post-import inspection, the saved
scene was reopened and native actor/instance verification passed again. A
separate driving smoke check then confirmed source-surface support and forward
movement before ending Play. This recovery does not establish that the engine's
intermittent physics crash is fixed. No frame-rate benchmark was performed.
