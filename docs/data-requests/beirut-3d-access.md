## Beirut 001: positioning and reuse clarification

Recipient: info@salimalkadi.com, published on the
[author's contact page](https://www.salimalkadi.com/contact-1).

Subject: Beirut 001 — coordinate reference and web visualization permission

Hello Salim,

We are developing DYNAcity, an interactive Beirut urban visualization application,
and are evaluating your publicly downloadable Beirut 001 model. Thank you for
making this substantial resource available.

We downloaded the model from your project page. The file uses metre units and
local coordinates, with no geographic Earth anchor set. Could you share its
coordinate reference system, north orientation, and transformation or control
points for alignment to WGS84 / UTM zone 36N (EPSG:32636)?

Could you also confirm the applicable licence, attribution requirements, and
whether we may convert the model to glTF/3D Tiles, optimize and crop the geometry,
and serve the resulting geometry in a web application? Please distinguish
permission for private evaluation, public noncommercial display, commercial use,
and distribution of derivative assets if their conditions differ.

Is the January 2020 release still the latest version, and are there known coverage
limits or accuracy qualifications we should display?

We are seeking a no-cost option and are not requesting a paid order.

Thank you.

## Iconem / UNESCO / DGA: historical-centre survey access

Primary technical contact: contact@iconem.com, published on
[Iconem's website](https://iconem.com/research/).

Institutional route: beirut@unesco.org, published on the
[UNESCO Beirut office page](https://www.unesco.org/en/fieldoffice/beirut).
Ask UNESCO to route the request to its Culture team and the responsible DGA
survey-data custodian. Do not assume the UNESCO office owns all reuse rights.

Subject: Access inquiry — Historical center - Beirut (2021) 3D survey

Hello,

We are developing DYNAcity, an interactive Beirut urban visualization application,
and would like to evaluate the UNESCO / DGA / Iconem surveys of Gemmayzeh,
Mar Mikhael and Karantina. We found “Historical center - Beirut (2021)” in
Iconem's catalogue, where opening it states that access is restricted and can
be requested.

Could you confirm who can authorize access and whether a no-cost evaluation
or research-use route is available to our project? We can provide further
project and affiliation details for eligibility assessment; we do not assume
that we qualify for institutional access.

For a small initial evaluation area, could you provide or arrange access to:

- A textured mesh (OBJ with MTL and images, glTF/GLB, or 3D Tiles), or clarify
  whether the available deliverable is a coloured point cloud instead.
- Its coverage polygon, acquisition dates, resolution, accuracy report, horizontal
  CRS, vertical datum, local origins and georeferencing transformation.
- The applicable terms for private evaluation, public interactive web display,
  conversion/tiling/decimation, self-hosting, and downloadable derivative geometry.
- Required attribution, permitted audiences, expiry or access restrictions,
  and whether any fees apply.

The historical capture would be labelled by survey date and would not be
presented as current building conditions. A limited sample is sufficient to
check geometry, textures and alignment before seeking broader access.

We are seeking a no-cost option. Please do not initiate paid work or a subscription.

Thank you.
