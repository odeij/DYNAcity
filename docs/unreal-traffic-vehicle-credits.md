# Traffic vehicle credits


The models below are modified CC BY 4.0 assets. Changes: upstream geometry simplification and meshopt compression; local decompression, orientation, ground alignment and Unreal material conversion. BMW also omits the authored display plinth and uses uniform unit scaling. Body materials support per-vehicle paint.


- [BMW E36 M3](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/bmw-e36-m3-762b90efd3dc413cb61c3e3c8097b8ac) by [Martin Trafas](https://sketchfab.com/TinoD2), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Downloaded from the credited [carros redistribution](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pedrosoandre/carros/main/assets/bmw-e36-m3.glb).

- [Toyota Corolla (KE80)](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/toyota-corolla-ke80-2b30faa611e64177a94a69098d27d1fb) by [PHAC](https://sketchfab.com/phac), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Downloaded from the credited [carros redistribution](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pedrosoandre/carros/main/assets/toyota-corolla.glb).


These named vehicle models are used for visual variety. Equal BMW/Toyota sampling is an illustrative choice, not a survey of Beirut makes. The original player Ferrari and generic commercial-vehicle credits remain in the existing library manifest.


Paint category weights: [Axalta global 2025 report](https://www.axalta.com/content/dam/New%20Axalta%20Corporate%20Website/Public/Documents/US/axalta-global-automotive-color-popularity_2025.pdf). Scope is new vehicles produced worldwide, not Lebanon vehicles on the road. RGB swatches are illustrative.
