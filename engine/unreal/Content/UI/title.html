About DynaCity

An explorable 3D model of Beirut, built for the DynaCITY urban-forecasting project. Drive or fly through the city, find places and look at mapped streets, greenery and street furniture.

—municipal road length modeled
—municipal terrain from source data
—road segments exported

Prototype. Geometry comes from OpenStreetMap and cached reference data and is partly approximate. Traffic is synthetic (SUMO). Nothing here is a forecast or a validated measurement.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Built with Unreal Engine.

Background street photos: KartaView, July 2018, CC BY-SA 4.0, © Grab and KartaView contributors. Each is paired with a DynaCity render from the photo's recorded position.
