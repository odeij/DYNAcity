DYNAcity: digital twin of Beirut

Explore a 3D model of Beirut's terrain, streets, buildings and trees, and inspect its modeled streets by driving a test vehicle.

Prototype Built from OpenStreetMap and cached reference data; geometry is partly approximate and traffic is synthetic. Not a forecast or a validated measurement.

Measured against the 12 cached municipal production areas. Terrain, road geometry and continuous driving are separate checks. Approximate DEM extensions are excluded from source-terrain coverage. Street furniture is incomplete in OSM.
